MONSOON SYNOPTIC CLASSES

Four Monsoon Regime Classes

Distinct error distributions call for targeted AI post-processing strategies.

ACTIVE REG-ACT

Large-scale monsoon activity, strong low-level cross-equatorial southwesterly jet, and enhanced rainfall along the monsoon trough.

Typical NWP Failure Mode: Underestimates extreme windward orographic rain along Western Ghats.
ATMOS AI Specialist: Sharpens convective cores & applies slope aspect condensation multipliers.
BREAK REG-BRK

Monsoon trough shifts north to Himalayan foothills. Marked suppression of rainfall across central and peninsular India.

Typical NWP Failure Mode: Spurious "drizzle bias" (predicting 2-10 mm where zero rainfall falls).
ATMOS AI Specialist: Applies zero-hurdle probability filter to eliminate false precipitation.
DEPRESSION REG-DEP

Monsoon depression or cyclonic disturbance moving west-northwest from Bay of Bengal into Central India.

Typical NWP Failure Mode: Tracks low-pressure center well but under-predicts heavy rainband intensity.
ATMOS AI Specialist: Vorticity-guided heavy-rain realignment and extreme probability calibration.
TRANSITION REG-TRN

Atmospheric state undergoing transition between active and break phases with competing wave modes.

Typical NWP Failure Mode: High forecast variance and ensemble spread across operational runs.
ATMOS AI Specialist: Soft gating increases Global Model baseline weighting to prevent overfitting.
